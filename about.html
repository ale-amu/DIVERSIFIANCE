To provide insight and express the opinions of others, a series of interviews were done to get the opinions of some students, teachers and educators in the IT field. This does not reflect the opinion of all but a select number of individuals who decided to participate.

    Name: Paula - Student

  • Why did u join the I.T. program? - I joined the I.T. program at my high school because I took an interest in the art aspect in technology. I found the techniques that are used in art to be very interesting.
  • Do you do you see yourself in an IT or stem field career with your learning today impacting it in some way? - I definitely see myself in the I.T. field as a career for my future. I enjoy everything that I am currently learning with I.T. (specifically Multimedia) and I can’t wait to show the world what I can do.
  • Did you have access to resources that aided your love for multimedia? - Not really, If I had more equipment and resources for the IT field, I would create more complex art pieces and continue to learn about the hardware and software involved.

    • Name: Rachel - Hispanic American Student

  • Why did you join the I.T. program? - I always wanted to learn graphic design and photoshop and I felt like it would be a useful skill for me to do.
  • Do you think race/ ethnicity will make it difficult for you to succeed in your future or career? - I would like to think that it wouldn’t, but of course there is bias in most things especially in the job market. But you know it also depends on what company you’re going into the people u work with. (But I would like to think that it probably won’t)
  • Do you think there should be more diversity and inclusivity in the IT field? - Absolutely, because there should be diversity in every field, especially because it is a very stable opportunity that should be offered to everyone. No matter what they are.

    • Name: Jessinta (Student)

  • Why did you join the IT program? - I joined the IT program because back when I was younger, my mom got into the IT field and I thought that the profession she was doing was very empowering for women.
  • Do you want to go into an IT field/career? (Why or Why not)? - Yes, I think it's very important for women of color to go into a field where minority aren’t seen.
  • Do you think race or ethnicity will make it more difficult for you to succeed in your future career/field? - I do think race will make it more difficult because there are more people of my race starting to be more competitive in STEM fields.
  • And do you think this is a good thing? - Yeah, it shows that there is more ownership and we are starting to be seen more
  • Do you think that if you were exposed to resources that built your interest from a young age, do you think things might have been easier for you to explore more empty fields or careers? - Yes, because currently as I was just exposed to the IT world about two years ago, I don't really know much careers in the IT field so I feel like if I was exposed to it earlier, I would have known about it and already known what I'd like to do and not like to do.

    Educators and Others in I.T. Field

    Name - Tracy Andrews ( Instructional Technology Admin)

  • Have you ever felt that the IT field was a male dominated field? - I definitely feel like it used to be as I first started, I was a teacher first and I taught math and science and during that time when I was trying to decide what I wanted to do it was definitely predominantly male. And though I went to college originally for engineering, I was told that women don't belonging in engineering and I actually believed them at the time. But I definitely think that it has gotten better.
  • What are some challenges you faced? - It was, part of it was I came from an area where there wasn't a lot. I didn't grow up with a lot so there wasn't a great deal of exposure to the possibilities that were out there. But I think that there tends to be, boys tend to be giving those opportunities more such as gaming and stuff like that and then they start to realize how many opportunities there are which isn’t always the case for us.
  • Why do you think there should be more diversity and inclusivity in the IT world? - Yeah, I think it's important to expose more individuals to the opportunities that are out there. Our entire world now is built on technology and there's so many different jobs and new opportunities and many of them don't even require at college education, though some do and I think it would be great to expose younger students of all types of different backgrounds two different opportunities that might give them that edge up.

    Name: Pullum (Multimedia Teacher)

  • Were you exposed to resources that built your interest from a young age, do you think things might have been easier for you to explore more empty fields or careers? - Yes, in college is where I developed an interest for this field, you know colleges have the newest equipment, most of the newest technology, so I was able to start of with those resources.
  • Have you ever felt that the I.T. field was a majority male dominated field? -Yes, it always has been but there have been some practices to help with that, but it has not been successful yet.
  • Why do you think there should be more diversity and inclusivity in the IT world? - It is a proven fact that whenever you have a diverse population (workplace or school) it raises the intellect/ intelligence of everybody involved. And I think that we are missing a lot of unique ideas and technology because we are not involving people with those type of backgrounds.

    • Name: Ms. Bowling (Multimedia teacher)

  • Have you ever felt that the I.T. field was a majority male dominated field? - Yes, I have felt that the IT field was a majority male-driven field. Every job I have had outside of teaching and within the IT realm have been comprised of mostly males.
  • Were exposed to resources that built your interest from a young age, do you think things might have been easier for you to explore more empty fields or careers? - Unfortunately, I was not exposed to resources that enabled me to excel in my field. I was not exposed to any computer-based (or technology-based) art until I went to college...and I paid for that!
  • Do you think there should be more diversity and inclusivity in the IT world? - I think there should be more diversity and inclusivity in the IT world because it builds a stronger team that can offer a wider range of solutions using a variety of backgrounds and experiences.
  • What are some resources you would share with students? - Some resources that can help students build an interest in IT (art-related) fields are the Adobe Education Exchange. There are a lot of free lessons and tutorials to assist in learning various Adobe programs, which can lead to jobs! Also, practicing various computer graphics and video editing programs and apps using YouTube tutorials can also help individuals excel at their craft and give them a competitive edge in an IT-related field.
